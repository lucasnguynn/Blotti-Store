03 / THE BLOTTI QUALITY JOURNEY

Little details. Real standards.

Quality begins long before the first wear. Here is how we are shaping Blotti's approach to fabrics, construction, care and product information.

01
THE MATERIAL

Made to feel considered

We begin with fabric specifications for hand-feel, stretch and comfort, then evaluate samples before any final production decision.

02
THE EVERYDAY TEST

Care is part of quality

Our planned evaluation considers wash performance, dimensional change and appearance after care, with relevant ISO methods as reference points.

03
THE FINISHING TOUCH

Small details matter

Construction choices such as seams, labels, fastenings and finishing are reviewed as part of our babywear design process.

04
THE INFORMATION

Clarity comes first

We want parents to understand sizing, composition, care instructions and the evidence behind any product claim.

OUR STANDARDS JOURNEY

Evidence, not empty promises.

We are preparing for applicable Vietnamese textile-safety requirements and exploring independent testing methods. Product-level test reports and certifications will be shown here only after they are issued and verified.

When can I shop the collection?+

Our first collection is being introduced as a preview. The launch date and availability will be shared when product details and ordering arrangements are finalized. The current website doesn't accept orders.

How does Blotti's Fit Finder work?+

Our early Fit Finder suggests a starting size from your baby's measured height and fit preference. It is a guide, not a substitute for approved garment measurements. Learn more in our size guide.

What goes into Blotti's quality approach?+

We focus on material specifications, construction, wash performance and appropriate chemical-safety requirements. Independent certificates will be linked only after they have been issued. View our quality notes.

What will shipping and exchanges look like?+

We've outlined the intended service experience for our Vietnam launch, including delivery estimates and a proposed exchange process. These policies will be finalized before orders begin. See shipping and returns & exchanges.

How do I care for Blotti pieces?+

Use the care label on your garment as the primary guide. Our fabric care notes share helpful general habits for babywear.
