Is Blotti available to purchase right now?+

Not through this website. This is a storefront prototype based on a business proposal. Products, stock, shipping, checkout, and pricing beyond the proposed hero price are illustrative.

What is the Learning Fit System?+

Blotti proposes combining basic measurements, customer feedback, and improved size recommendations over time. The interactive size finder here is a simple rule-based demonstration, not a validated recommendation engine.

Are Blotti products certified?+

No issued certification or finished-product report has been supplied for this concept. Our Proof Lab distinguishes required/considered testing from evidence that has actually been obtained.

How do I choose a size?+

Try the Fit Finder, starting with your baby's measured height. The result is an illustrative suggestion; check final garment measurements and brand-specific tolerances before any real purchase.

What about delivery, returns, or exchanges?+

Real shipping, returns, warranty, and exchange policies are not established here. They would need to be defined and published before launch.
